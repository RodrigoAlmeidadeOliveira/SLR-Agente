                                                                                                              V. Related Work
                                                                                     Process mining has been extensively researched [6], [7], [8]
                                                                                  over the last two decades, which was surveyed by van der Aalst
                                                                                  et al in [1]. In concurrency, the alpha algorithm [5] made the
                                                                                  ﬁrst step. Under the framework of ProM [2], more techniques
                                                                                  [5], [8] were approached.
                                                                                     Sequence mining [9] is a part of process mining. Many
                                                                                  works of it are also under research [7]. The study of sequential
                                                                                  pattern mining basically started from the traditional data
                                                                                  mining. R Agrawal et al. proposed AprioriAll, AprioriSome
                                                                                  and GSP [3] methods on the base of a classic data mining
                                                                                  algorithm Apriori. Then M. Zaki et al. also proposed SPADE
                                                                                  [10] as a Apriori-based method. J Pei, et al continue to work
                                                                                  on this ﬁeld and PreﬁxSpan [11] ran as a representative.
