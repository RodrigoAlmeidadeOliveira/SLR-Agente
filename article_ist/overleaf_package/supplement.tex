This supplement lists the 456 publications of the corpus: identifier,
year, role (\emph{coded set} for the 306 that remain from the coded list
of 338; \emph{added} for the 150 studies added after the human re-screen),
and the family pattern counted in the classification matrix of the main
text (Appendix B there). TF-PM is process mining (IC4a), TF-ST a stochastic
model (IC4b), TF-FC forecasting (IC4c), and TF-MSR repository mining
(IC4d); the 76 title-only studies carry no family mark. The same list is
\texttt{included\_studies\_506.csv} in the replication package
(\href{\zenododoiurl}{Zenodo DOI~\zenododoi}), which also stores, for each
identifier, the bibliographic record and the screening decisions.
