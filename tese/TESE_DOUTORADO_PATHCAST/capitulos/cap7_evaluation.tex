\chapter{Empirical Evaluation}
\label{ch:evaluation}

This chapter reports the empirical evaluation of PATHCAST on the public corpus
defined in Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology}. Section~\ref{sec:e2c} presents the
cross-paradigm experiment E2-C (RQ4/RQ4b): weekly throughput derived from Markov
simulation compared against the IJF audit of MCS\_ML, and lead-time CRPS in days
against baseline B6. Artefacts are generated by
\texttt{scripts/run\_e2c\_batch.py} in the PathCast replication repository
(v0.8.0).
